## 2026-06-10 - Focus Visible Indicators
**Learning:** Implementing custom `:focus-visible` indicators globally ensures keyboard navigators have a high-contrast experience without disrupting mouse users. Setting an `outline-offset` prevents the outline from overlapping with the element's natural bounds, and matching the primary brand color (#2563eb) maintains design consistency.
**Action:** Always define custom `:focus-visible` styles with an appropriate offset and brand color for all focusable elements in new projects.
